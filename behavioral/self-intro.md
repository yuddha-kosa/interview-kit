## ~90-105 second version (use this as the default)

"I'm a Staff Software Engineer at RapidAI. Our platform processes around 10 million medical scans a year across roughly 2,000 hospitals, running more than 25 AI modules — at its core it's a data pipeline that orchestrates those modules and gets their results out reliably. I've spent the last few years building most of the distributed-systems foundation underneath it, essentially from zero to one.

A few things mattered most in that build. On data: I led the evaluation of CockroachDB and Yugabyte against Postgres, and given real constraints — third-party dependencies we didn't control, and infra footprint on-prem — made the call to stay on Postgres, split by ownership rather than forcing one database to do everything. On availability: I designed the multi-region HA and failover architecture behind our 99.99% availability target across those 2,000 hospitals and 6 regions. And the platform had to stay abstracted from the individual AI modules and flexible enough to support real clinical workflows — that's the push-to-PACS work, coordinating result delivery from multi-region cloud down to on-prem hospital systems, with the data secured both in transit and at rest along the way.

As the platform matured, we needed real visibility into it, so I led the OpenTelemetry rollout — defining tracing standards across teams — which cut incident triage time by about 30 minutes per engineer. And once we were deploying at that scale, deployment itself became a real bottleneck, so I took the initiative, outside my formal role, to redesign our CI/CD process around ArgoCD, which cut release-related incidents by more than 80%.

Underneath all of it, I cared about consistency — I built a shared Go platform and abstraction layer that's now used by 40-plus services, with standard templates so engineers build the same way instead of everyone reinventing it.

[Close — customize per company, don't leave this generic:] That's what's brought me here — I'm looking at [Plenful / Mercor] because ___."
